Supplier Mandatory Requirements

We only cooperate with real and legitimate suppliers. The following documents are mandatory:

  • Valid Bill of Lading (BL)
  • Official SGS test report for commodity quality
  • Company business registration certificate

Minimum Order Quantity

  • Aluminum Ingot: 500 MT minimum
  • Electrolytic Copper: 100 MT minimum
  • Petroleum Coke: 1000 MT minimum

Payment Terms

We accept L/C at sight or TT. We will never pay any advance deposit before documents verification.

Note: Brokers without direct access to goods are not encouraged to submit forms. Please do not send repeated spam submissions.
