About Us

We are a direct purchaser focusing on bulk industrial raw materials, including aluminum ingot, electrolytic copper and petroleum coke.

We maintain long‑term stable procurement demand. We only work with genuine suppliers who can provide complete, verifiable supporting documents.

All offers will be manually reviewed after receiving your supporting documents. If your proposal matches our procurement standard, we will contact you via email for further negotiation.
